## Claims to avoid

The site's charts and company logos are real. The news, insider, analyst and
earnings signals in the terminal are invented sample content demonstrating the
product. Do not post anything implying the signal feed is live — say the
product is coming, or point at the demo as a demo. Getting caught overclaiming
on day one costs more than it gains.

Also skip: price targets, "guaranteed", "100x", "cannot go down", or anything
that reads as financial advice.
